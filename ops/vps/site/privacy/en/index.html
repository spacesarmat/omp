Privacy policy

Published: 18 November 2026

OMP ("the app") is a media player that plays video from a TorrServer that you run yourself on your home network. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Your media and history stay on your network

The app connects to your own TorrServer over your local network and plays the media you have added to it. Your media files are not uploaded to the developer. Playback history (the watched file and position) is stored on your device and in your own TorrServer, so it can be shared across your devices; it is not stored on the developer's servers.

2. Optional diagnostics (can be turned off)

By default the app may send diagnostic data to the developer's server to help fix problems:

  • a small number of usage events (such as "installed" and "signed in"), and
  • crash reports (error message, stack trace and recent app log lines).

Log lines are scrubbed of secrets (access tokens, cookies, network addresses, magnets, file paths) before sending, but they may still contain media titles - for example when the app corrects a release's category. Diagnostic data is used only to fix problems, not to build a viewing profile. You can turn diagnostics off at any time in Settings; when off, no usage events or crash reports are sent.

3. Metadata (TMDB)

To show posters and descriptions for the items in your library, the app may query The Movie Database (TMDB) with the title of an item. These requests contain the title query and are subject to TMDB's own privacy policy. No personal account data is sent to TMDB.

4. No account required

No account or sign-in is required to play your own media. The core playback functions work without providing any personal information.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, precise location or contacts, and we do not sell personal data. We do not build a viewing profile; as noted in section 2, diagnostic logs may incidentally include media titles and are used only to fix problems.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose media player and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is this page: https://omp-player.online/privacy/en. The Russian version is https://omp-player.online/privacy.
