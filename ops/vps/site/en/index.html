QUESTIONS

OMP questions, answered

What is OMP?

OMP (Open Movie Player) is a free, open-source media player for LG webOS TVs, Android TV and Android phones. It plays movies and series from TorrServer on your home network, and your phone is the remote and a second screen. No ads.

How do I watch torrents on my TV?

TorrServer fetches a torrent piece by piece and streams the video over your network, so a movie starts before the download is complete. Install OMP on the TV, connect TorrServer and pick a movie or an episode on your phone.

Do I need a separate TorrServer?

Not necessarily. On an arm64 Android phone, OMP downloads TorrServer from GitHub and runs it for you. The phone, the TV and the server must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Which TVs and boxes are supported?

LG TVs on webOS 4.0 or later (roughly 2018 and newer, no root needed), and Android TV and Google TV on Android 8 or later. Xiaomi, Sber and Yandex boxes take the same APK. Samsung (Tizen) is not supported yet.

How do I install OMP?

The easiest way is from the phone: Settings → “Install OMP on TV”. The phone finds LG and Android TV devices on the network and installs OMP itself. Files for a manual install are in the latest GitHub release; see the install guide.
