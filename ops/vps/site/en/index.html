HOW IT WORKS

Three steps
to the opening credits.

The TV and the phone on one home network.
OMP shows you the rest.

Full install guide on GitHub
  1. 01

    Install OMP

    Download the build for LG webOS, Android TV or Android from the latest release.

  2. 02

    Connect TorrServer

    Point OMP at your server, or turn on the built-in TorrServer on the phone — no separate PC.

  3. 03

    Link the phone and the TV

    A QR code on LG, or a 6-digit code on Android TV. Pick a film — and sit down.
